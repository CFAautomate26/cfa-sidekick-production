# Document Templates

Skeletons for the documents the playbooks call for. Square brackets are
fill-ins. Lines marked **⚖** are legally load-bearing — do not delete or
soften them without a reason. Every issued document goes in the employee's
file the same day, and anything beyond a routine warning should be generated
fresh through a counsel session (these are floors, not ceilings).

---

## 1. Written warning

> **CONFIDENTIAL — Written Warning**
> Employee: [name] Role: [role] Date: [date]
> Issued by: [leader name/title]
>
> On [date(s)], [specific factual description — times, what happened, who
> observed it. Facts only, no characterizations].
>
> This does not meet the standard set out in [policy/handbook section, or the
> plain expectation, e.g. "your scheduled shift commitments"].
>
> Going forward, we expect: [specific, observable change].
>
> **⚖** We are committed to supporting you in meeting this expectation. If
> there are circumstances affecting your ability to do so that you would like
> to discuss — including anything you may need support or accommodation for —
> please tell [leader/Operator].
>
> **⚖** Further incidents of this nature may result in further disciplinary
> action, up to and including termination of employment.
>
> Employee signature (acknowledging receipt, not necessarily agreement):
> ______ Date: ______
> [If refused: "Employee declined to sign; copy provided." + witness initials]

The **⚖** accommodation invitation matters: it protects the discipline record
if a disability or family status issue surfaces later.

## 2. Final written warning

Same structure as above, plus, after the incident description:

> **⚖** This is a **final written warning**. You have previously received
> [list: verbal coaching on (date), written warning dated (date)] regarding
> [the same/similar conduct]. Any further incident may result in the
> termination of your employment.

## 3. Performance improvement plan (PIP)

> **Performance Improvement Plan — [name]**
> Period: [start] to [end — typically 30/60 days] Review cadence: [weekly]
>
> 1. Current gaps: [specific, measurable — "order accuracy at X% vs. standard
>    of Y%", not "bad attitude"].
> 2. Expected standard: [measurable target per gap].
> 3. Support provided: [retraining, shadow shifts, check-ins — must be real].
> 4. Check-in log: [date / notes / initials rows].
>
> **⚖** Failure to achieve and sustain these standards may result in further
> action, up to and including termination of employment.

## 4. Termination letter — without cause

> Dear [name],
>
> This letter confirms that your employment with [legal entity name,
> operating as Chick-fil-A Wharncliffe & Wonderland] ends effective [date].
> This decision is not a reflection of any single incident; we thank you for
> your contributions. *(Adjust honestly — do not recite performance reasons
> in a without-cause letter.)*
>
> You will receive:
> - **⚖** [X weeks] pay in lieu of notice pursuant to the *Employment
>   Standards Act, 2000* [+ severance pay of $X if applicable];
> - **⚖** all outstanding wages and accrued vacation pay to [date];
> - **⚖** continuation of your benefits (if any) through the ESA notice
>   period;
> - your Record of Employment, issued within 5 calendar days.
>
> [If offering above-ESA money:] In addition, and conditional on your signing
> and not revoking the enclosed Full and Final Release by [date — give at
> least 5–7 days and state they may seek independent legal advice], we will
> provide [additional consideration]. **⚖** The ESA amounts above are yours
> unconditionally and do not depend on signing anything.
>
> Please return [uniform, keys, etc.] by [date]. Questions about this letter
> or your final pay: [contact].

**STOP-gate**: any termination letter for an employee with 3+ years, any
protected circumstance, or any above-ESA package → counsel review before it
is handed over. Do not draft your own release — releases come from counsel.

## 5. Accommodation — request for functional information

> Dear [name],
>
> Thank you for letting us know about [the circumstance, in the employee's
> own general terms]. We want to support you and meet our accommodation
> obligations under the *Human Rights Code*.
>
> **⚖** To do that, we need information about your functional abilities and
> restrictions — **not your diagnosis or medical condition**. Please have
> your health care provider complete the attached functional abilities form /
> confirm: (a) any restrictions on tasks, hours, or environment; (b) whether
> these are temporary or permanent; (c) expected duration if temporary.
>
> We will keep this information confidential and use it only to assess
> accommodation options. We may follow up to discuss options; you are welcome
> to propose options as well.

## 6. Modified work offer (WSIB or medical return)

> Further to [FAF/medical note dated X], we are pleased to offer you the
> following modified duties consistent with your restrictions: [list duties,
> hours, wage]. This offer is effective [date]. Please confirm acceptance by
> [date]. If you believe any duty is inconsistent with your restrictions,
> tell us immediately and we will review it with you.

(For WSIB claims, copy the offer to the WSIB case file.)

## 7. Investigation report skeleton (harassment/violence)

1. Mandate: complaint received [date/how], allegations summarized,
   investigator, interim measures taken.
2. Process: who was interviewed and when; documents reviewed (messages,
   video, schedules).
3. Allegation-by-allegation findings: evidence for, evidence against,
   credibility notes, finding **on a balance of probabilities**.
4. Conclusion: harassment as defined by OHSA/policy — substantiated /
   unsubstantiated / partially.
5. **⚖** Written results to complainant and respondent: a summary of findings
   and of any corrective action taken or to be taken (not the full report).

## 8. Job abandonment letter

> We have attempted to reach you on [dates/methods] after your absence from
> scheduled shifts on [dates] without notice. Please contact [name] by
> [date — give 2–3 business days]. **⚖** If we do not hear from you by that
> date, we will treat your employment as having been abandoned, effective
> [date], and will issue your final pay and Record of Employment.

## 9. Employment agreement — termination clause health check

Not a template — a checklist for reviewing any agreement before relying on it:

- [ ] Without-cause clause limits entitlements to ESA minimums **explicitly
      including** notice/pay in lieu, severance pay (if applicable), benefit
      continuation, and vacation pay — and says nothing that could pay less.
- [ ] With-cause clause tracks the ESA "wilful misconduct" standard — a bare
      "we may terminate for just cause without notice" clause is what
      *Waksdale* kills, voiding the whole termination scheme.
- [ ] No "at any time" / "sole discretion" language (post-*Dufault* risk).
- [ ] Fresh consideration if signed after the start date (a raise, a bonus —
      something new; continued employment is not consideration).
- [ ] Signed **before** the first day worked.
- [ ] If any box is unchecked → treat as **no enforceable clause** (common
      law exposure) and have counsel re-paper on the next promotion/raise.
