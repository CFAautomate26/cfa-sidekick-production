# August 2026 Leadership Action Plan — Win the Window. Own Friday Night.

**Chick-fil-A Wharncliffe & Wonderland FSU (#30029)** · London, Ontario
From the July performance review meeting, August 14, 2026

**August priorities:** 1) Drive-thru guest experience · 2) Window engagement · 3) Dining room cleanliness · 4) Speed of service

Context adopted by the leadership team: underlying profitability ~9% (excluding marketing credit); sales $467K (−28%, traffic-driven — checks held at $22); drive-thru 52% of sales, −33%, lowest-rated channel; last year's food cost inflated by tariffs; the 0.51% food-cost gap likely an inventory count error (pending verification), not under-portioning.

## Focus 1 — The window standard (every car, every time)

Principle: **a car arriving at the window is a guest walking up to the counter.**

- **Dedicated window person** on every lunch/dinner peak from Mon Aug 17.
- **Greet every car** — standard greeting written and posted by Aug 18, trained to all DT shifts by Aug 22.
- **Promote the mobile app at the window** (8,900+ cars/month; app pre-payment is the payment-speed answer; outside payment person not viable at current volume). Target ≥5 conversions/day.
- **Offer BOGs at the window** — ~3.5 attached items/order, hits marketing line not food cost, no P&L downside until 2027. Target 328 → 450+ redemptions; push sides, beverages, desserts, kids meals.
- **QR flyer in every DT bag** — Allegra creative brief Aug 20; in bags week of Aug 25.
- **Accountability:** ignoring a window guest = ignoring a counter guest. Document → verbal → escalate. Effective immediately.
- **Back-of-house speed:** trim line time (1:52) and fulfillment (1:16). Total 4:23 → ≤3:45; departure 132s → ≤120s. Speed of service before headcount.
- **Drinks prepped with food** — bagging process posted before Monday's mobile dine-in launch.
- **No scheduled breaks 11 AM–2 PM** to protect the 1 PM rush.

## Focus 2 — Deliver Friday night

Friday: busiest night, lowest-rated day (70% OSAT), already the most-staffed day — won by deployment and leadership presence, not headcount.

- Strongest shift leaders on Friday dinner, positions locked, dedicated window person all night. Test nights: **Aug 21 and Aug 28**.
- Pre-shift huddle with the 70% on the board.
- Dining room cleanliness on a named-owner rotation through dinner.
- Friday scorecard reviewed Mondays (first: Aug 24): sales, total time, departure rate, window-standard misses, cleanliness walks.

## Blindspots

1. **82% AHA is a milestone, not the finish line** — at 82, ~1 in 6 batches still runs past hold. September CEM spicy taste/temp is the real test; set 85 next.
2. **"Inventory count error" is still a hypothesis** until the Aug 31 verified count — and portion satisfaction at 61% is real guest feedback either way.
3. **The window moment is getting crowded** — greeting + app + BOG + payment in ~30 seconds. One offer max per car; watch departure rate weekly; if it climbs, trim the script, not the greeting.
4. **Friday is an all-day problem** — Friday lunch is the week's biggest block (~$10.5K). Name separate leads for Friday lunch and dinner.
5. **Cutting 2–7 PM hours during a launch month** — window standard + mobile dine-in land the same weeks. Standards first, hours second; if service dips, pause the trim, not the standard.
6. **Third-party delivery is parked without a decision** — Uber −40%, DoorDash −35% (~$25K/month), no action item. Put 3P on the September agenda: keep, fix, or deliberately de-prioritize.
