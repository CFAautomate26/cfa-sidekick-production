What removing does

Applicants are told their application is kept for a set number of months after the hiring decision if they are not hired ({{ months }} months for anyone applying today), and that you then remove it. Nothing is removed automatically: it happens when you press the button below.

  • Removed: name, email, phone, the yes or no answers, availability, referrer, experience, written answers, leaders' notes, the notes on scores, any AI copy of the answers, and the applicant's private link.
  • Kept: the bare record (posting, stages and dates, levels), so the numbers stay true.
  • Kept three years by law: every decision notice, with the applicant's name, until it is three years old. After that its wording and the name are removed too.
  • Never removed: postings and the kept copies of them, anyone who was hired, and any application on hold.

Change the number of months in People settings. Each applicant keeps the number they were told. The retention period is a choice to confirm with a lawyer: a human rights application can be made up to a year after a decision, and sometimes later.
