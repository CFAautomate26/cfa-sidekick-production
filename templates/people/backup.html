{{ icon('lock') }}This file holds private information about people. Keep it somewhere only you can open. Never put it in email, a group chat, Slack or a shared drive.

What is in the file

Everything on People pages, as one file: who has access, settings, every version of our standards, leader reviews and observations, job postings and applications, the record of what was sent to and received from the AI service, and the activity log. The Team Hub backup does not include any of this, and this file does not include Team Hub.

Private links that applicants hold are stored scrambled, and the key that scrambles them stays on the server: it is not in the file.

Each download is written in the activity log.

Where to keep it, and for how long

A backup is a copy from one day. When old applications are later removed here, your older files still hold them. Keep only the newest copy and delete the ones before it, unless a complaint or legal claim is under way: then keep every copy and call the lawyer.

Putting a backup back

Restoring from this file is not built in this version. The file is a safety copy: if the server's disk were ever lost, the records would be rebuilt from it by hand.
