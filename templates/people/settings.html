AI drafting for leader reviews

The AI prepares a draft from the evidence for the reviewer to check. It never rates on its own, never recommends a consequence and never compares leaders. The reviewer rates each standard first, and a person makes every decision.

Before you switch it on, these should be true

  1. Leaders have been told in writing that an AI tool helps prepare a draft and that a person decides.
  2. Our standards have been checked, so every standard is about behaviour at work.
  3. You have looked at the AI provider's data terms: no training on our information, how long it is kept, where it is stored, and that it can be deleted.
  4. A first review cycle has been run without AI.

What the AI is sent

The reviewer's observations for the period and the leader's own reflection. That means the observations that count toward the review, the reflection only if the leader sent one, and with them the wording of our standards. First the leader's name, the names of people on the Team Hub roster, email addresses, phone numbers and he/she words are taken out. A plain word list looks for wording about health, family, age, religion, origin, gender, time away or a complaint, and a note, or a part of the reflection, that it catches is not sent. A word list cannot catch every way of saying these things, so reviewers are told to leave such notes out themselves. It is never sent the leader's name, title or area, the reviewer's own ratings, or anything from Team Hub activity (checklists, to-dos, training or read receipts). What was sent and what came back is kept on record.

Wording you can use to tell leaders
Starting [date], we are introducing a regular leader review. Here is how it works. Your reviewer writes down what they have observed about your work against our written standards, which you already have [say where]. If we ask you for one, you add your own reflection. An AI tool then helps prepare a first draft from those notes and your reflection. Your name and the names of people on our team are taken out before anything is sent to the AI service, which is run for us by an outside provider [and may process information outside Canada]. Nothing from your Team Hub activity is used. The AI does not decide anything about you. Your reviewer rates each standard themselves, checks the draft, and a person makes every decision. You will see your review, talk it through with [reviewer or Operator], and can add your own comments, which stay with the review. [Time you spend on your reflection and your review is paid time.] Reviews are confidential to you, your reviewer and [Operator name]. [They are kept on our Team Hub server, which a hosting company runs for us and which may be outside Canada.] Time away on a protected leave or under an accommodation is not counted against you. If anything is affecting your work that you would like support or an accommodation for, please tell [name] at any time. Questions about the process or how your information is handled: [contact].

Square brackets are facts for you to fill in or confirm. Only send what is true for this store.

AI assist for hiring

The AI compares an applicant's written answers with the posting's criteria and suggests a level for the leader to check. It never rejects, advances or ranks anyone. This switch only makes AI possible: it also has to be switched on for each posting while that posting is still a draft, so the posting says so from the day it goes live.

Before you switch it on, these should be true

  1. A lawyer has looked at using AI to help assess applicants here, before you rely on it.
  2. You have looked at the AI provider's data terms: no training on applicants' information, how long it is kept, where it is stored, and that it can be deleted.
  3. Every posting that uses AI says so in the posting itself, before anyone applies.
  4. Leaders who score applications know the AI suggestion is a second read: they score first, and a person makes every decision.

What the AI is sent

The posting's criteria and the applicant's written answers. First the applicant's name, the names of people on the Team Hub roster, email addresses, phone numbers and he/she words are taken out. It is never sent the applicant's name or contact details, their availability, how they heard about us, their experience, the 18-or-over and work-entitlement answers, or interview notes. An answer that may mention health or an accommodation is not sent at all. It runs only for applicants who applied while the posting was showing its AI statement. What was sent and what came back is kept on record.
