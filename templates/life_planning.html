Life Planning Workshop

Chick-fil-A Wharncliffe & Wonderland · London, ON
A 7 week life planning workshop with direct mentorship from the Operator, designed for anyone who is serious about their life's journey and maximizing growth in every area. Together we build a vision and plan for the whole of your life: Career, Family, Friends, Community Impact, Faith, and Legacy. Before you sign up, make sure you can commit to the following:
  • 7 weeks: we cover one life planning section per week.
  • Weekly meetings for the full 7 weeks. No lates, rescheduling, or misses (excluding emergencies).
  • Worksheet completed before each meeting to maximize growth during your time.
7 spots per group: 5 Team Members and 2 Leaders.
Once this group completes the workshop, a new 7 week group will launch.
